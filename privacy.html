Privacy Policy

Adventure Church YouTube Publishing Helper
Effective date: November 16, 2026
Contact: video@theadventure.church

This privacy policy covers the internal Google OAuth client used by The Adventure Church (Draper, Utah) to publish and update sermon videos on the church’s own YouTube channel (“Adventure Church”, channel ID UCqGOmbxCSJNczDwETpEgK9Q).

Who we are

The Adventure Church is a local church. The helper is operated by the church’s video / production team for church ministry use only. It is not a public consumer product and is not offered to third-party customers.

What the helper does

With authorization from a church Google account that can manage the Adventure Church YouTube channel, the helper may:

  • Upload edited sermon videos to that channel
  • Set or update title, description, tags/keywords, thumbnail, playlist membership, and privacy status
  • Update livestream broadcast title, description, and related video metadata so they match that week’s service notes

Data we access

Through Google’s YouTube Data API, the helper accesses only data needed for those publishing tasks on the church channel, including OAuth tokens for the authorized church account and video metadata the church already controls on YouTube.

The helper does not request access to your personal Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or other Google products beyond the YouTube scopes granted for this channel work (youtube and youtube.upload).

How we use data

OAuth tokens and publishing logs are stored on church-controlled systems used by the video team so Sunday sermons can be uploaded and labeled consistently. We use this data only to operate and troubleshoot church YouTube publishing. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as required to run Google’s OAuth / YouTube services or as required by law.

Data retention

Refresh tokens are kept only while the helper needs ongoing access to publish to the channel. Tokens can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings for the authorizing account. Publishing logs may be retained for operational history and then deleted when no longer needed.

Children

This helper is not directed at children and is not used to collect personal information from children.

Your choices

A church account holder who authorized the helper can revoke access at any time in Google Account → Security → Third-party access (or the equivalent Google controls). After revoke, the helper cannot call the YouTube API for that account until someone signs in again.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the effective date on this page and keep the current version at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the helper: video@theadventure.church.
